State of money

Do you know your cash position six months out? Positive cash flow every month? The capital growth will take?

State of time

Does your time go where it should? Any time to think and plan? What's important, or just what's loud?

State of systems

Sales process that produces? Hiring process that works? Follow-up that keeps customers? Clear expectations, always?

State of people

Clear performance expectations? Can your team say what you're worth to a customer? Are you investing in them?
